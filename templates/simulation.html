RESEARCH INTERPRETATION

What this simulation can tell you

Scenario analysis can reveal how the trained model's prediction changes when inputs are perturbed. This can support sensitivity analysis and model-behavior research.

It cannot demonstrate that an intervention on a real student would produce the simulated outcome. Observational model associations should not be converted into causal claims.

RESEARCH BOUNDARY

Scenario results are hypothetical model outputs. They describe sensitivity of the trained model to input configurations and do not establish causal effects, individual future outcomes or clinical conclusions.
