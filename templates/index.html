ABOUT THE PLATFORM

A research instrument
with explicit boundaries.

StressIntel PRO investigates how heterogeneous student-related signals can be combined into an interpretable stress-risk estimate.

+
Designed for

Research, experimentation, model analysis and educational demonstrations.

−
Not designed for

Medical diagnosis, clinical decisions or replacement of professional support.

≠
Interpretation ≠ causality

SHAP contributions describe model behavior and do not prove causal effects.

◇
Development data

The included sample dataset is synthetic and is not population evidence.
